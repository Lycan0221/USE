# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 19, 2026**

Tonghe Yexing Wire & Cable Co., Ltd. (“Tonghe Yexing,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and handle information submitted through our Facebook and Instagram lead forms and related business communications.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry or lead form, we may collect information such as:

- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Company name
- Country or region
- Business type
- Cable type, specification, and required quantity
- Expected purchase time
- Other information you voluntarily provide during your inquiry

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

- Respond to your product inquiries
- Recommend suitable wire and cable products
- Prepare quotations based on your specifications and quantities
- Contact you by phone, WhatsApp, Messenger, or email
- Follow up on your purchasing requirements
- Provide product information, samples, order support, and after-sales service
- Improve our customer service and sales processes
- Maintain necessary business and transaction records

We use your information primarily for legitimate business communication relating to your inquiry or potential purchase.

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Where reasonably necessary to provide our services, information may be shared with service providers or business partners that support activities such as communication, quotation preparation, logistics, order processing, hosting, or customer service.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or a lawful request from a competent authority.

## 4. Facebook, Instagram and Other Platforms

If you submit information through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, Meta Platforms may also process information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

If we communicate with you through WhatsApp, Messenger, email, or other third-party platforms, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for purposes such as responding to inquiries, preparing quotations, maintaining customer relationships, processing orders, meeting business record requirements, and complying with applicable laws.

Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted or securely disposed of.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no electronic communication or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request that we:

- Tell you what personal information we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your personal information where applicable
- Stop using your information for certain communications
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on your consent

You may contact us to make such a request.

## 8. Marketing Communications

We may contact you regarding your inquiry, quotation, requested products, or related business opportunities.

If you no longer wish to receive marketing or follow-up communications from us, you may tell us at any time and we will take reasonable steps to stop such communications.

## 9. International Business Communications

As we serve international customers, information you provide may be processed or accessed in countries or regions other than your own where reasonably necessary for business communication, quotation, order processing, logistics, or customer support.

We will handle such information in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, services, or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be identified by the effective date shown at the top of this page.
